Description

A duplicate IP address conflict occurs when two or more devices in the OT/PCN segment respond to the same IP — via ARP replies, DHCP lease collisions, or simultaneous flows from different MAC addresses.

Before classifying the conflict type, the query performs a MAC Status pre-check against a 30-day history lookup. A brand-new MAC (never seen before) triggers CRITICAL immediately. A MAC that changed its associated IP also triggers CRITICAL immediately. In both cases, notification must happen before any further analysis.

For known MACs, three conflict vectors are evaluated: ARP conflict (same IP, two MACs), DHCP lease collision, and dual-source traffic. The fields MacStatus and ConflictType classify each result automatically, with ImmediateNotify flagging cases requiring instant action.

MAC Status always overrides conflict-type severity. NEW or IP_CHANGED → CRITICAL regardless of how the conflict was detected. The 30-day lookback runs in parallel with the 5-minute sliding window for live conflict detection.

Splunk Query — Duplicate IP Detection · Simplified & Performance-Optimized



index=network (sourcetype=arp_logs OR sourcetype=dhcp_logs OR sourcetype=network_logs)

| eval src_ip  = coalesce(src_ip, assigned_ip),
       src_mac = coalesce(src_mac, client_mac),
       ConflictType = case(
           sourcetype="arp_logs",     "ARP_CONFLICT",
           sourcetype="dhcp_logs",    "DHCP_COLLISION",
           sourcetype="network_logs", "DUAL_SOURCE_TRAFFIC")


| stats dc(src_mac) AS UniqueMacs
        values(src_mac) AS MacList
        values(ConflictType) AS ConflictType
        BY src_ip
| where UniqueMacs >= 2


| lookup crown_jewels.csv   ip AS src_ip   OUTPUT asset_name, criticality_tier,
                                                     mac AS cj_mac
| lookup mac_ip_history.csv mac AS src_mac  OUTPUT known_ip


| eval
    IsCrownJewel = if(isnotnull(asset_name), "YES", "NO"),
    MacStatus    = case(
                       isnull(known_ip),              "NEW",
                       known_ip != src_ip,             "IP_CHANGED",
                       true(),                        "KNOWN"),
    CritReason   = case(
                       IsCrownJewel = "YES",          "CROWN_JEWEL",
                       MacStatus    = "NEW",           "NEW_MAC",
                       MacStatus    = "IP_CHANGED",   "IP_CHANGED",
                       true(),                        "DUAL_MAC"),
    Severity     = case(
                       IsCrownJewel = "YES",          "CRITICAL",
                       MacStatus   != "KNOWN",        "CRITICAL",
                       mvcount(ConflictType) > 1,    "HIGH",
                       true(),                        "MEDIUM")

| table src_ip, MacList, known_ip, asset_name, criticality_tier,
         IsCrownJewel, MacStatus, ConflictType, CritReason, Severity
| sort  0 - Severity

Severity Logic

CRITICAL 🔴
Rule 1 — 2+ MACs same IP: always CRITICAL, no exceptions.
Rule 2 — Crown Jewel IP or MAC matches lookup → CRITICAL.
Rule 3 — NEW or IP_CHANGED MAC → CRITICAL.
HIGH
Known MAC, not CJ. ARP conflict or dual-source traffic. Potential ARP spoofing / MITM on known OT device.
MEDIUM
Known MAC, not CJ. DHCP lease collision only. May be misconfiguration or failover. Validate with Operations.

What We Look For
CJ Does the conflicting IP or MAC appear in the Crown Jewels lookup? If yes → CRITICAL immediately. Crown Jewel assets demand the highest response priority — engage OT Operations before any further step.
DUAL Are there 2 or more MACs responding to the same IP within the 1-minute window? If yes → CRITICAL always. No exceptions — regardless of whether MACs are known or unknown.
MAC Is the conflicting MAC brand-new (no 30d history) or did it change its IP? Either → CRITICAL. Investigate via SNMP switch (port), ARP table (gateway), DHCP leases (hostname). Ask OT if a VM was cloned or a device was moved.
OT Always contact OT Operations before drawing conclusions. The conflict may be a planned VM migration, device replacement, or maintenance — confirm before escalating to Incident.
